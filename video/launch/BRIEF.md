## Intent

A relaunch video for Antípoda.fm round 2: the product got a real 3D globe (drag inertia, pinch/wheel zoom, keyboard), a first-run guide, and camera choreography. The video demos the product the way its own first-run guide teaches it — grab the planet, name a place, tune — then the signature bore through the Earth as payoff, ending on the URL. Silent: kinetic type in the product's fonts carries it, readable with sound off. Voice: hushed, precise, slightly awed — no hype.

## Assets

- Real product footage captured with Playwright recordVideo from the running app (the actual UI, no mockups).
- Product identity: ink #0A0B10, bone #F2EEE3, signal orange #FF4D00, phosphor #7CFFB2; Fraunces display, IBM Plex Mono data.

## Customizations

- The video mirrors the in-app guide's three steps as its demo beats.
- End card: antipoda-fm.vercel.app over the drifting planet.

## Notes

- Silent by design: `music: none`, no narration, no sfx. Type and motion do the work.
- The product's own motion language is the reference: eased camera flights, dashed signal-orange rings, phosphor earned only by live audio.
